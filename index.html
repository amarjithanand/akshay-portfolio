    01 / PROFILE

    About

    I am a PhD Research Scholar in Computer Science at the National Institute of Technology Calicut. My doctoral research focuses on handwritten mathematical and scientific expression recognition and understanding.

    My work explores adapting vision-language models to challenging visual inputs using parameter-efficient fine-tuning methods such as LoRA. I am particularly interested in evaluating performance beyond aggregate metrics by studying ambiguity-critical cases, conducting controlled experiments, and analysing model errors.

    My broader interests include multimodal learning, computer vision, OCR, robust model evaluation, and practical machine-learning systems.

    02 / RESEARCH

    Selected research work

    Research themes, methods, and engineering contributions from my work on handwritten mathematical expression recognition.

    Manuscript under reviewR01

    CaMER: Confusion-aware Adaptation of Vision-Language Models for Handwritten Mathematical Expression Recognition

    Adapts Qwen2.5-VL-3B using LoRA and studies recognition behaviour on ambiguous handwritten mathematical expressions.

    Overall ExpRate51.49% → 54.61%
    Ambiguity-critical ExpRate32.18% → 48.72%
    Research details

    Research question

    How can a vision-language model be adapted to improve recognition of handwritten mathematical expressions, with particular attention to visually ambiguous cases?

    Method

    The work applies LoRA-based adaptation to Qwen2.5-VL-3B and uses an ambiguity-focused evaluation protocol alongside aggregate recognition metrics.

    Evaluation

    Experiments use MathWriting, CROHME, and HME100K. The reported results are specific to the evaluated setup; they should be interpreted alongside the manuscript's full experimental protocol.

    Implementation

    29.93 million trainable parameters (reported as 0.79% of the model), with PyTorch and PyTorch Lightning training/evaluation workflows.

    Manuscript status: under review. A venue, DOI, preprint, or public code link has not been supplied.

    Evaluation designR02

    Ambiguity-focused evaluation

    Designed an evaluation protocol that examines confusion-critical expression pairs alongside aggregate performance to better understand recognition behaviour on ambiguous inputs.

    Research details

    Focus

    Complement overall Exact Match Rate with targeted analysis of ambiguity-critical cases and inspect where model predictions diverge from expected expressions.

    Methods

    Controlled comparisons, qualitative inspection, ablation studies, and systematic error analysis.

    Research engineeringR03

    HMER training and evaluation pipeline

    Built reproducible training and evaluation workflows for handwritten mathematical expression recognition across established benchmark datasets.

    Research details

    Engineering

    PyTorch and PyTorch Lightning pipelines with bfloat16 mixed precision, gradient accumulation, checkpointing, and experiment resumption.

    Compute environment

    Experiments were run using NVIDIA A100 80 GB and V100 32 GB GPUs.
